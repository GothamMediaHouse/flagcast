For players

The app in every cart

One player per cart keeps score, and the same phone knows the course.

  • No download, no account

    Scan the card on the cart and you’re in. It runs in the Flagcast app on iPhone or in any phone’s browser, so nobody is left out.

  • Scoring on one screen

    Each hole on one screen: tap in the scores, mark a pick-up, and move on. Scores are saved on the phone and sent as soon as there’s signal.

  • Yards to today’s pin

    GPS yards to the front, the back and the flag, drawn on the green. The pro sets the pin sheet each morning, so it’s today’s pin, not the middle.

  • Fly the hole

    A drone-style flyover from tee to green with yardages along the way and round every dogleg. Or fly all 18 back to back.

  • Everyone on the map

    Every group live on the course map, with who’s ahead and who’s behind, so it’s easy to keep pace.

  • Live leaderboard

    Updates seconds after a score goes in. Split by flight, net and gross, with movers marked as they climb.

  • Side games

    Stableford, Vegas and Wolf scored for each group as they play. Kept in points, for bragging rights.

  • Long drive and closest to the pin

    Players enter their own distance on the contest hole, with GPS to help. The pro checks it before it goes up on the board.

  • Par 3 cams in your pocket

    Watch the par 3 cameras live, rewind a couple of minutes, and replay any shot.

  • Score on the lock screen

    On iPhone, your score and place stay on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, without opening the app.

  • In your club’s colours

    Your club’s logo and colours on every screen the players see.

  • Try it before the day

    No code needed for the demo: open the app and tap Try a demo to play a sample round.

For the golf pro

Pro tools on the course

The same app with a pro PIN turns into a run-the-day toolkit, so the pro never has to go back to the shop.

  • Every cart, live

    See where every group is and which cards have gone quiet.

  • Fix a score in a tap

    Correct any card from the course, and the boards update straight away.

  • Set the day’s pins

    Drop each pin on the green, or copy yesterday’s sheet in one tap.

  • Check contest entries

    Long drive and closest to the pin entries wait for an OK, a change or a removal, so only checked numbers reach the screens.

  • Post news to the screens

    A quick message on every screen at the club: the shotgun start, the lunch call, the awards time.

  • Go live from your phone

    Stand at any hole and send your iPhone’s camera to the screens and the players’ Cameras tab.

Flagcast Manager

The whole day from the pro shop

On a Mac or Windows PC in the pro shop, on an iPhone or iPad out on the course, or in any browser.

  • Set up the course in minutes

    Search for your course, then fill in every hole from the map, doglegs included.

  • Teams, flights and handicaps

    Best ball, stroke play, scramble or Stableford, with handicaps, flights, net and gross.

  • Cart cards and signs

    Print a QR card for every cart and signs for the tee, in your club’s look.

  • Every code on one card

    The tournament code, pro and marshal PINs and the cart codes, together where you need them.

  • Day-of checks

    TVs on, cameras sending, quiet carts nudged in a tap, before the first group tees off.

  • Contests your way

    Choose whether golfers enter their own numbers, how many get checked, and whether there’s a prize in points.

  • Pick what each TV shows

    The leaderboard, a par 3 cam, the contests or your sponsors, one choice per screen.

  • Sponsors on every screen

    Hole sponsors on the camera feed, sponsor slides between leaderboard updates, and logos in the app.

  • Lock the results

    Lock the final scores and print the results sheet for the awards and the club.

The screens

Something to watch at every turn

TVs in the clubhouse, the tent and the grill room rotate through the scores, the cameras and your sponsors.

  • Live leaderboard

    Rotates between flights, with movers marked and sponsor slides in between.

  • Par 3 cam

    Every tee shot on the par 3s live, with an instant replay and the hole sponsor on screen.

  • Hole-in-one

    When someone makes an ace, every screen at the club stops for it.

  • Contest boards

    Long drive and closest to the pin, updated as soon as an entry is checked.

  • Fundraising total

    A live total for your charity on every screen, revealed at the dinner.

  • The club’s own TVs Coming soon

    Apps for Apple TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Roku. Pair each TV once with a code and it joins every tournament by itself.
