The live leaderboard

Scores land seconds after they’re entered on the course. Split by flight, net and gross, with sponsor slides in between.

Cameras

Every par 3 shot, live

Every tee shot on the par 3s plays live, with an instant replay and the hole sponsor on screen.

  • Instant replay of every shot
  • Hole sponsor credit on screen
  • On every phone too, in the Cameras tab

  • Take-over on every screen
  • Replay ready in seconds
  • Saved for the awards dinner
