How it works

You run the tournament.
We run the show.

One call to plan it, and our crew handles the rest on the day. Nothing for your staff to set up or learn.

Weeks before

Plan it

Send us your format, field and sponsors. We map the camera spots, screens and run of show with your pro shop.

Morning of

We set up

Cameras on the par 3s, screens in the clubhouse and tent, a QR card on every cart. Done before the first group arrives.

On the course

Play live

One player per cart keeps score on their phone, with GPS yards to the pin. Leaderboards update in seconds, and every par 3 shot plays on the screens.

Awards dinner

The big finish

Final results, contest winners, your sponsors, your fundraising total and a highlight reel of the day.

Something to watch at every turn

Big screens around the club rotate through the leaderboard, the par 3 cams and your sponsors, and break in when something big happens.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Do players need to download anything?

    No. Players scan the QR code on their cart sign and score in the phone's browser, with GPS yards, the course map and side games included.

    Are the side games for money?

    No. Stableford, Vegas, Wolf and the rest are kept in points, just for bragging rights. Flagcast doesn’t take bets or handle any money.

    What formats and handicaps do you handle?

    Stroke play, best ball, scramble, shamble, Stableford and match play, with full or percentage handicaps, multiple flights, net and gross.

    What if the course has poor cell signal?

    Scores are saved on the phone and sent as soon as there is signal, so nothing is lost. We bring our own network for the cameras and screens.

    Do you bring the screens?

    Yes. We bring TVs and stands, or we can use the club’s own screens. We can also bring an LED wall for the tent or dinner. Apps for Apple TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Roku are coming soon, so the club’s own TVs can join in with a code.

    Where do you work?

    Anywhere in the US. We bring the crew and the gear to your course, and travel is quoted with your date.

    How far ahead should we book?

    Spring and summer weekends fill first, so two to three months ahead is best. We can sometimes do it with a couple of weeks’ notice.
