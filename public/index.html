How it works

    Questions

    Good to know

    Do players need to download anything?

    No. Players scan the QR code on their cart card and score in the phone's browser.

    What formats and handicaps do you handle?

    Stroke play, best ball, scramble, shamble, Stableford and match play, with full or percentage handicaps, multiple flights, net and gross.

    What if the course has poor cell signal?

    Scores are saved on the phone and sent as soon as there is signal, so nothing is lost. We bring our own network for the cameras and screens.

    Do you bring the screens?

    Yes. We bring TVs and stands, or we can use the club’s own screens. We can also bring an LED wall for the tent or dinner.

    Where do you work?

    Anywhere in the US. We bring the crew and the gear to your course, and travel is quoted with your date.

    How far ahead should we book?

    Spring and summer weekends fill first, so two to three months ahead is best. We can sometimes do it with a couple of weeks’ notice.
