The Flagcast app

Scoring players actually use

Scan the card on the cart and you’re in. No accounts and no passwords. The Flagcast app on iPhone, or any phone’s browser, so nobody is left out.

Getting started

Scan the card. Start playing.

Every cart gets a card with a QR code. One scan opens your team’s scorecard, already set up with your names, your handicaps and the course.

  • No app store, no account, no password
  • Works on iPhone and Android
  • The Flagcast app on iPhone adds the lock screen and more

Scoring

Each hole on one screen

The hole, the yards and every player’s score fit on one screen. Tap in the strokes, mark a pick-up, and move on to the next tee.

  • Net and gross worked out for you
  • Scores saved on the phone when there’s no signal, sent when there is
  • Your pro can fix a card from the shop
GPS

Yards to today’s pin

GPS yards to the front, the back and the flag, drawn right on the green. Your pro sets the pin sheet each morning, so “to pin” means today’s pin, not the middle.

  • Front, pin and back on every hole
  • Pin positions set by your pro each day
  • On every hole of the course map
Flyovers

Fly the hole before you hit

A drone-style flyover from the tee to the green, with yardages along the fairway and round every dogleg. Or fly all 18 back to back before you tee off.

  • Made from the course map, for every hole
  • Yardages along the line of play
  • Great on the range while you wait

The course map

Everyone on the map

Every group live on the course map, with who’s ahead and who’s behind. Easy to keep pace, and to know when to wait on the tee.

  • Each group’s hole, live
  • Group ahead and group behind
  • Only during the tournament, and staff can hide a group
Leaderboard

The live leaderboard, in your pocket

The same leaderboard that’s on the clubhouse TVs, updated seconds after a score goes in.

  • Split by flight, net and gross
  • Movers marked as they climb
  • Contest leaders too
Side games

Games inside the game

Stableford, Vegas and Wolf, scored for each group as they play. Kept in points, for bragging rights.

  • Scored automatically from the scorecard
  • Points only, never money
  • Group standings as you go

Contests

Enter your own long drive

On a contest hole, players enter their drive or their distance to the pin, with GPS to help. The pro checks each entry before it goes up on the screens.

  • Long drive and closest to the pin
  • GPS or typed in
  • Only checked entries reach the boards

Cameras

The par 3 cams, in your pocket

Watch the par 3 cameras live from anywhere on the course. Missed it? Rewind a couple of minutes or replay any shot.

  • Live from every camera hole
  • Rewind and replays
  • Your pro can go live from any hole with a phone
Lock screen

Your score without opening the app

On iPhone, your team’s score and place stay on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island while the phone is in your pocket.

  • Updates as scores come in
  • Through how many holes, and your place
  • Turn it off any time in Settings
