Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version: Flagcast keeps only what it needs to run golf tournaments. We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you around the web. Ask us and we’ll delete your information.

Who we are

Flagcast runs live scoring, leaderboards, big-screen displays and course cameras for golf tournaments, through flagcast.app and the Flagcast apps. Questions about this page: hello@flagcast.app.

What we store

  • Club staff accounts: your email address and the club you belong to. If you set a password, we keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy, never the password itself.
  • Signing in with Apple or Google: we receive your email address and an account number from Apple or Google to recognize you next time. We don’t receive your Apple or Google password, contacts or anything else.
  • Tournaments: what the club enters: the tournament name and date, the course, team and player names, handicaps or GHIN numbers when the club adds them, scores, contest results, sponsors, and the photos or logos the club uploads.
  • Players’ phones: scores entered on a cart scorecard. If a group turns on location for hole distances, the phone uses it for yardages, and the organizers may see where groups are on the course during the event. Locations are deleted when the tournament ends.
  • Running the service: basic records such as when an account signed in and what changed in a tournament, so clubs can see who did what and we can fix problems. Sponsor views and taps are counted for the club’s report, without identifying who tapped.

How we use it

Only to run the tournaments: show scores and leaderboards, send sign-in codes and club notices by email, and help clubs with their events. Players’ names and scores appear on the public leaderboard and on the club’s screens, since that is the point of a live leaderboard.

Who else sees it

  • The club running the tournament, and people the club gives access to.
  • Anyone with the tournament’s leaderboard link sees names and scores, and GHIN numbers are never shown publicly.
  • Services that run Flagcast for us: Cloudflare (hosting and storage) and our email provider (to send sign-in codes). They handle data only to provide that service.

We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t use advertising or cross-site tracking.

Keeping and deleting

Clubs can hide or remove their tournaments. To delete your account or a player’s information, email hello@flagcast.app from the address on the account, and we’ll do it within 30 days. Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes and sessions after 90 days.

Children

Flagcast is for clubs and tournament players. Junior players may appear on a leaderboard when their club or event adds them, and parents can ask us to remove a junior’s information at any time.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we’ll update the date above and tell club accounts by email.
