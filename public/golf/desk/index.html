Setup

Format

Handicaps on the Teams tab are each player’s course handicap. The allowance is applied to it, and strokes fall on the holes by stroke index. A picked-up ball counts as net double bogey. Ties are broken on the back 9, 6, 3 and last hole. Side games let each cart play skins, a Nassau and birdies among themselves; the scorecard works out who owes who, and Flagcast never takes or holds any money.

Course

Fills in par, yardage, stroke index and every set of tees with its rating and slope.

Photos golfers see

A cover photo shows when golfers open the tournament on their phone. A hole photo shows on that hole’s page. Photos save as soon as you add them.

Tees (for GHIN handicaps)

Add each set of tees with its course rating and slope from the scorecard or the USGA course rating database. Then give players their Handicap Index on the Teams tab and Flagcast works out each course handicap: Index × Slope ÷ 113 + (Rating − Par).

Teams

Each team gets its own scorecard code and QR card. Start hole is for a shotgun start. For a scramble, set the team handicap. Type a player’s Handicap Index (use + for plus handicaps, like +1.2) and pick their tees to have the course handicap worked out; or leave Index empty and type the course handicap yourself.

One team per line, columns split by tabs or commas: Team name, Flight, Start hole, Player 1, Handicap 1, Player 2, Handicap 2, and so on (up to 4 players). Copy the rows straight out of Excel or Google Sheets.

Scores

Fix any score here. Changes save as you leave each box and show on every screen. Type 0 for a picked-up ball, clear the box to remove a score.

Contests

Closest to the pin is measured in feet (4.5 = 4 ft 6 in). Long drive in yards. Results go on the screens as soon as you add them.

Set the contest’s hole and name, then Save before entering results.

Screens and sponsors

Announcement

Par 3 cameras

Cameras are set up by Flagcast. Pick “Par 3 camera” for a screen and it shows the hole’s camera (or a drawing of the hole when there isn’t one).

Par 3 replays

Players watch replays on their phones (under each par 3 on the live leaderboard), and you can put one on every screen. Save replay plays the hole’s camera back from 25 seconds before you press it, so the camera’s HLS link must keep a recording window (DVR). Or paste a clip link (.mp4 or .m3u8), for example from a replay system. Golf pros and marshals can save replays from the Flagcast app too.

Sponsors

Pick where each sponsor shows. Hole, camera and contest spots use the hole numbers. The link and offer show on the players’ phones, and taps are counted for the report. Logos are small pictures (PNG or JPG, under 140 KB).

Staff and playlists

Golf pros and marshals

They sign in to the Flagcast app with the tournament code and their PIN. A golf pro can fix scores, switch screens, take over the screens and nudge a cart. A marshal can only post contest results.

Tournament code

Screens at the club

Add each TV or LED wall and pick what it plays, in order. Open its link full screen on that TV. A screen with an empty list shows everything.

Schedule

Shows on the Schedule page of the screens.

Report

The GHIN file has each player’s GHIN number, tees, rating and slope, hole-by-hole scores and the adjusted gross score (each hole capped at net double bogey, picked-up holes counted as net double bogey), ready for the club’s handicap chair to post. Scramble scores can’t be posted for handicaps.

Sponsors

Screen time counts every minute a sponsor was on a connected screen. Phone views count each time a player saw it on their scorecard; taps count opens of their link.
